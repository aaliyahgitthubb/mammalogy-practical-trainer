Mammal Vault v2.1.0
- Added a clearly visible Edit Species button directly on every species in Manage Species & Images.
- Edit Species can correct common name, scientific name, family, notes, and identification clues.
- Edit Species functions are exposed correctly to the page buttons.
- Rebuilt Blur/Cover dragging with a desktop mouse + touch fallback instead of relying on pointer capture.
- Blur and Cover boxes share the same drag controller and can be moved anywhere within the image.
- Resizing remains available from the lower-right handle.
- Existing data.js and embedded_images.js are intentionally not included; keep your existing copies.
