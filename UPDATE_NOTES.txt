v1.9.4 - Flashcard image attachments

Adds the agreed image feature to flashcard creation/editing:
- Include an image from the selected species library.
- Choose a specific species image by thumbnail.
- Image placement defaults to No image.
- Placement options: No image, Separate image card, Front of card, Back of card.
- Separate image cards appear as their own study step in Flashcard Mode.
- Existing cards remain text-only unless edited.
- Flashcard question bank remains on the creation/edit page.

Preserve data.js, embedded_images.js, and images/ when applying this update.
